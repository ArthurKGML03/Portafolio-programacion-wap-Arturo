Introduction:

The project seeks, first of all, to prepare a secure testing environment by selecting and configuring a suitable vulnerable virtual machine. Next, a comprehensive reconnaissance of the target system will be performed, scanning and enumerating running services to identify potential attack vectors.

Objective:

Demonstrate the ability to apply theoretical computer security knowledge in a practical environment, by conducting a detailed walkthrough and explaining the techniques and methodologies used to compromise a virtual machine.

Activity description:

This activity has an approximate duration of 07 hours. Throughout the entire process, every step of the attack will be documented in detail, recording commands, results, and screenshots. In addition, an explanatory video of the walkthrough will be created by recording the screen and explaining each step in detail. A fundamental aspect of the project is the explanation of the theoretical computer security concepts related to the attack, linking the techniques used with theoretical foundations.

VIDEO

Conclusion:

This digital attack has successfully demonstrated the application of theoretical cybersecurity knowledge within a controlled environment.

By interacting with the target machine (SnakeOil), it was proven how unmitigated vulnerabilities allow a system to be compromised through the use of various tools, such as:

1: Linux commands

2: Burp Suite

3: Gobuster

Through the phases of reconnaissance, exploitation, and privilege escalation, absolute control of the system was obtained. It is important to highlight that, although the aforementioned tools were designed for ethical use, they can also be employed maliciously.

Therefore, as a fundamental lesson, the software of any system must always be kept updated to prevent the execution of old vulnerabilities or exploits.
